Locations FAQ

GTA VI locations: quick answers.

Does this GTA VI locations page show an interactive map?

No. It is an image gallery of settings and places named in Rockstar material. The gallery does not plot coordinates, routes, distances, or boundaries.

Where is GTA VI set?

GTA VI is set in the fictional state of Leonida, which includes Vice City. Other officially named destinations include the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park.

What is the latest official GTA VI update?

On October 29, 2026, Rockstar announced a 30-track original score album by Justice, coming December 11, 2026, and released “Pusher” featuring Gesaffelstein. Rockstar announced these details in its October 29 Newswire post.
