What the evidence supports

Names are confirmed.

Rockstar’s official GTA VI material identifies Leonida as the state and Vice City as a city in its setting. It also names the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga.

These entries record place names and the official source checked. They do not claim exact placement, borders, routes, scale or distance. The list will only gain geographic pins when a usable source supports them.

Locations FAQ

What is known about the GTA VI map?

Has Rockstar published the complete GTA VI map?

No complete official map is presented here. Rockstar’s public material names places, but this guide does not claim verified map positions, routes or borders.

Where is GTA VI set?

GTA VI is set in the fictional state of Leonida, which includes Vice City. Other officially named destinations include the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park.

Are the diagram’s locations to scale?

No. The original schematic groups officially named places to help browse them. It does not show geography, exact placement, distance, boundaries or scale.
