Quick answer · US prices

GTA 6 prices are confirmed in the US.

Take-Two announced a US price of $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. These are US dollar prices; they are not a promise of the amount charged in every country or storefront.

STANDARD EDITION

$79.99 USD

Grand Theft Auto VI base game.

ULTIMATE EDITION

$99.99 USD

Base game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade.

Prices announced by Take-Two Interactive on June 24, 2026. Always confirm the final price and terms on your platform’s store page.

Compare editions

What changes between Standard and Ultimate?

Standard Edition includes Grand Theft Auto VI. Rockstar says Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately after redeeming their game code.

Ultimate Edition includes the game and the Ultimate Edition Upgrade. Rockstar describes the upgrade as a collection of premium vehicles, weapons, apparel and other items connected with Jason and Lucia’s story.

The official edition listing names additional in-game items, but this guide does not infer gameplay value or recommend which edition to buy. Check the current edition contents in the linked Rockstar information before checkout.

Platforms & availability

Where GTA VI pre-orders are listed

Rockstar’s current pre-order information lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The official support page names the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store and Rockstar Store as storefronts, alongside select retailers in eligible countries.

Rockstar publishes separate country lists for each storefront. Availability is not universal: more countries may be added subject to platform policies, local regulations and age ratings. The available price and currency can vary by country and store; applicable taxes and the final checkout total depend on the market and seller.

Digital code regions can matter. Rockstar says a purchase made in a different region must match the region on the platform account, and recommends buying from a retailer in the account’s country to avoid activation issues. Xbox codes are listed as not region-locked in Rockstar’s platform guidance; check that guidance for current details.

Take-Two says eligible pre-orders and purchases made before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack. Digital pre-orders also include a free month of GTA+. Rockstar says digital preloads begin November 12; the physical code-in-box version is also available from that date to support preloading. Check the offer terms and your store listing, as availability is market-specific.

A PC pre-order is not listed in the official platform and pre-order information linked here. This guide does not announce or predict a PC version.

Straight answers

GTA 6 price and pre-order FAQ

Is the GTA 6 price confirmed?

Yes. Take-Two announced US prices of $79.99 for Standard Edition and $99.99 for Ultimate Edition on June 24, 2026. Other markets may show different local prices.

How much will GTA 6 cost in my country?

The $79.99 and $99.99 figures are the announced US prices. Check your own PlayStation, Xbox, Rockstar or eligible retailer listing for the current local currency price and checkout total. GTA SIXX does not convert those prices into unannounced regional amounts.

Can I pre-order GTA VI now?

Rockstar’s support page lists pre-orders through PlayStation Store, Xbox Store and Rockstar Store, plus select retailers in eligible countries. Availability can differ by storefront and country. Use the official store links above and confirm your region before purchase.

Is there a GTA VI pre-order bonus?

Take-Two says eligible pre-orders and purchases made before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack. Digital pre-orders also include a free month of GTA+. Check the official announcement and your regional store for terms and availability.

Which platforms have official GTA VI pre-orders?

Rockstar lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC pre-order is not listed in the official platform information cited on this page.

What is included in the GTA VI Ultimate Edition?

Rockstar says it includes Grand Theft Auto VI and the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, with premium vehicles, weapons, apparel and additional items. See Rockstar’s edition and version details for the current official list.

Is there a physical GTA VI Ultimate Edition?

Rockstar’s platform guidance says a physical Ultimate Edition is not available. It describes the physical Standard Edition as a code-in-box with no disc; Standard owners can buy the digital Ultimate Edition Upgrade after redeeming their game code.

Are GTA VI pre-orders available worldwide?

No single storefront list guarantees worldwide availability. Rockstar publishes separate country lists and says additional countries may be added subject to platform policies, regional regulations and age ratings. Digital code region compatibility can also depend on your platform account.

Where is the official Rockstar Games GTA VI pre-order page?

Rockstar’s official information is on its GTA VI pre-order details page and the Rockstar Games Store listing. Verify current country and price details there.
