Quick answer

Which vehicles are confirmed?

Rockstar’s official GTA VI media currently names five vehicles in edition-related content: the 95 Grotti Cheetah, 55 Vapid Stanier Sedan, Ganado Retro Build, Shitzu Squalo and 67 Vapid Dominator Buggy. This is a confirmed-name list, not the complete in-game vehicle roster.

Edition note: the 95 Grotti Cheetah, Ganado Retro Build, Shitzu Squalo and 67 Vapid Dominator Buggy are listed with Ultimate Edition benefits. The 55 Vapid Stanier Sedan is listed with the Vintage Vice City Pack. Availability depends on edition or promotion terms.

What kinds of vehicles are in GTA VI?

Rockstar has not released a full categorized roster. Its official launch and gameplay footage includes road vehicles and transport across land and water; the names above are the clearest individually identified vehicles in the public official media library we reviewed.

This guide will distinguish a vehicle visibly shown in a trailer from a vehicle officially named by Rockstar. Fan-made lookalike identifications and model guesses are not presented as confirmed entries.

Is this every vehicle in GTA 6?

No. It is a partial list of models Rockstar has named publicly. The full vehicle roster is not confirmed.

Are aircraft and boats confirmed?

They appear in official promotional footage, but this guide does not assign unverified model names or claim a complete category list.

Will GTA 6 vehicles be available in every edition?

Some named cars are connected to edition or pre-order benefit materials. Consult Rockstar’s current edition details for eligibility.
