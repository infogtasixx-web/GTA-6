The short version

What happens in GTA 6?

Jason and Lucia have always known the odds are stacked against them. When an easy score goes wrong, they are pulled into a criminal conspiracy across Leonida and must rely on each other to make it out alive. That is the official story premise; Rockstar has not published a full plot synopsis.

Spoiler note: this guide sticks to details Rockstar has officially shared and avoids inventing unrevealed missions or endings.

The protagonists

Jason Duval & Lucia Caminos.

Jason Duval

Rockstar describes Jason as someone who wants an easy life, but keeps finding things harder. He grew up around grifters and crooks, spent time in the Army, and later worked for local drug runners in the Keys. Meeting Lucia could change the course of his life.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia learned to fight from her father and has spent much of her life fighting for her family. After time in the Leonida Penitentiary, she is focused on making smarter moves and building the life her mother dreamed of. A life with Jason may be a way forward.

The official synopsis frames the pair’s relationship around trust, risk and survival. See the character guide for the other named people revealed so far.

Story FAQ

What is confirmed about the plot?

Is GTA VI a direct sequel to GTA V?

Rockstar has not described GTA VI as a direct character sequel to Grand Theft Auto V. The announced story follows Jason and Lucia in Leonida.

Will GTA VI have two protagonists?

Rockstar’s official pages introduce Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as the central protagonists. Specific character-switching mechanics have not been fully detailed on the story page.

Is the GTA 6 story set only in Vice City?

No. Vice City is part of Leonida, and Rockstar’s synopsis says the criminal conspiracy reaches across the state. The announced world includes areas beyond the city.
