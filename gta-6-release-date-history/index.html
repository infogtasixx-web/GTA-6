Grand Theft Auto VI · Confirmed dates

GTA 6 release date history.

Rockstar first announced a 2025 release window, then set and later changed a specific date. Here is the verified timeline, with each milestone linked to Rockstar’s announcement.

Current release date ↓Check Rockstar’s official page ↗

Quick answer · Verified November 2, 2026

When does GTA 6 come out?

Rockstar Games currently lists Grand Theft Auto VI for November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This is Rockstar’s latest publicly listed date as checked on November 2, 2026.

The earlier dates below are part of the announcement history. They are not the current release date.

Straight answers

GTA 6 release date FAQs.

What is the current GTA 6 release date?

Rockstar’s official GTA VI page lists November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Check the linked official page for later changes.

What was the original GTA 6 release window?

Rockstar’s December 2023 Trailer 1 announcement said the game was coming in 2025. It did not specify a release day.

Was GTA 6 delayed from May 2026?

Yes. On November 6, 2025, Rockstar announced a November 19, 2026 release date, replacing the previously announced May 26, 2026 date.

Are the release dates based on rumors?

No. The milestones on this page come from Rockstar Games Newswire announcements and its official GTA VI page. Unannounced dates and rumors are not presented as confirmed.
