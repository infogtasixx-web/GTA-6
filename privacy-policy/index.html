GTASIXX · Site information

Privacy Policy.

A plain-language description of what this fan guide does—and what happens when you use its contact and media links.

Your information

How this site handles information.

GTASIXX is a static fan guide. Based on the current site code, it does not include an analytics service, account system, advertising tracker, or server-side contact form.

Contact form and email

The contact form validates the fields in your browser and opens a prefilled draft in your email application. It does not send the message to a GTASIXX server. Your message reaches infogtasixx@gmail.com only if you choose to send it from your email service. Your email provider will handle the message under its own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

The website code reviewed for this policy does not set first-party cookies or use browser storage for analytics. No analytics or advertising scripts were found. This describes the current website implementation and may change if site features change.

Fonts, images, and video

The pages load typefaces from Google Fonts and some artwork thumbnails from YouTube's image host. The homepage loads a YouTube video player only after you select a trailer. Requests to these third-party services are handled by their providers and may expose standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser request details. YouTube may also use cookies or similar technologies when its player loads, according to Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy ↗.

Links to Rockstar Games and other external websites take you away from GTASIXX. Review the privacy information of any external service you choose to visit.

Hosting and technical requests

The website is hosted as static files. The site code does not provide access to the hosting provider's operational logs or define how infrastructure requests are retained. For information about GitHub's handling of data, read the GitHub Privacy Statement ↗.

Children

The contact form asks anyone who chooses to use it for a name, email address, subject, and message; it does not include an age-screening step. Parents and guardians should supervise minors' use of the contact form.

Changes to this policy

If the website adds features that affect privacy, this page should be updated to describe them. This policy covers the implementation currently present in the site repository.

Questions

For questions about this page or the site, email infogtasixx@gmail.com.

GTASIXX.com is an independent fan-made website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
