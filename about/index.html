Quick answer

What is GTA 6?

Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI or GTA 6) is the next single-player open-world action-adventure game from Rockstar Games. It is set in the fictional state of Leonida, including Vice City, and follows protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Rockstar lists November 19, 2026 as the release date for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The world

Vice City is one part of Leonida.

Rockstar describes Leonida as a sprawling state with a return to modern-day Vice City. The official setting material also names destinations and regions including the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park.

Grand Theft Auto VI returns to the wider Vice City setting associated with the series while introducing Jason and Lucia’s contemporary story. The guide pages below organize the confirmed world, people, plot and vehicle information.
