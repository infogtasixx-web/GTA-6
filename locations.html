Quick answer

Where does GTA 6 take place?

Grand Theft Auto VI is set in Leonida, a fictional US state that includes modern-day Vice City. Rockstar has also named the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park. The confirmed places show a world beyond the city; a complete official map has not been published here.

Major city

Vice City

The neon-soaked urban heart of Leonida and a modern return to one of the Grand Theft Auto series’ best-known settings.

Island chain

Leonida Keys

A chain of islands and waterside communities. Jason is described as working for local drug runners in the Keys.

Port Gellhorn

An officially named Leonida destination beyond Vice City, introduced in Rockstar’s locations material.

Wetlands

A named Leonida area that points to environments outside the dense Vice City streets.

National park

Mount Kalaga National Park

A named park among the announced destinations in the state. Rockstar has not published a complete map legend on this page.

World guide

One state, distinct places.

Leonida is the broader fictional state; Vice City is a city within it. This distinction matters when describing the setting: GTA VI is not presented as taking place only in Vice City.

The official character page uses “Only in Leonida” to introduce both people and destinations. The names on this page are transcribed from Rockstar’s official GTA VI material. Unconfirmed map boundaries, neighborhood lists and travel distances are not treated as facts.
