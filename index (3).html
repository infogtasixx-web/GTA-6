What is GTA 6 about?

Grand Theft Auto VI heads to the state of Leonida, home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and the wild country beyond. Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. When an easy score goes wrong, they land on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, caught in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the state.

FAQ

GTA 6 questions, answered.

When is the GTA 6 release date?

Rockstar Games lists November 19, 2026 as the Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) release date. Its October 15, 2026 Launch Trailer also states the game arrives November 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There are 0 days left on the live timer above.

Which platforms is GTA 6 coming to?

GTA 6 launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar confirmed GTA 6 plays best on PS5. A PC release has not been announced.

Where is GTA 6 set?

The game takes place in the fictional US state of Leonida, which includes the neon-soaked Vice City.

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

The two protagonists are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. When an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves caught in a criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida, forced to rely on each other to make it out alive.

How many GTA 6 trailers are there?

There are four official videos: Trailer 1 (December 4, 2023), Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025), An Extended Look (August 27, 2026 — a 26-minute gameplay showcase that premiered on Netflix), and the Launch Trailer (October 15, 2026). You can watch all of them in the trailers section above.

Can I pre-order GTA 6?

Yes. Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026. The Standard and Ultimate Editions are available. Check the Rockstar store for current prices and regional availability.

Who makes GTA 6?

GTA 6 is developed and published by Rockstar Games, a label of Take-Two Interactive. It follows Grand Theft Auto V as the next main game in the series.

Was GTA 6 delayed?

Yes. The game was originally scheduled for May 26, 2026 and was moved to November 19, 2026. Rockstar's statement cited extra time needed "to finish the game with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve."

What time does GTA 6 unlock in my timezone?

This site counts down to 12:00 AM local time on November 19, 2026 in each timezone. Rockstar Games has not published an exact unlock time, so check official channels closer to launch.
