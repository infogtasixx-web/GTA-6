Justice’s GTA VI original score arrives December 11

Rockstar announced a 30-track adaptive score by Justice, due December 11, 2026. The reveal also brought out “Pusher,” featuring Gesaffelstein. This is the latest GTA VI Newswire announcement listed as of November 2.

Rockstar previews Leonida’s radio stations

Rockstar introduced selected in-game radio stations, including Flash FM, The Chamber, Afro Bank FM, Back Country, Cocoteo and Dirty South Classics. It also shares tracks from the separate 34-track Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album.

Rockstar Games: GTA VI music and radio ↗
October 15, 2026

Launch Trailer now playing

Rockstar released the official 90-second Launch Trailer on October 15, 2026, featuring the debut of Flower in a Hurricane by score composers Justice, featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. Rockstar’s announcement reaffirmed the November 19, 2026 release date.

August 27, 2026

An Extended Look — 26 minutes of gameplay

Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look premiered on Netflix, then went live on Rockstar's YouTube channel. The roughly 26-minute showcase — captured entirely in-game on PlayStation 5 — was the first time Rockstar showed real gameplay of Vice City, Leonida, Jason Duval, and Lucia Caminos in action.

Watch the Extended Look →Official GTA VI page ↗
November 6, 2025

November 19 release date is locked in

Rockstar Games announced that Grand Theft Auto VI would release on November 19, 2026. The updated date followed an earlier plan for May 26, 2026.
