People of Leonida

Meet the faces of GTA VI.

Jason and Lucia lead a cast of hustlers, artists, business owners and outlaws across Leonida. These profiles reflect characters Rockstar has officially introduced.

Official character profiles · Rockstar Games

Quick answer

Who are the GTA 6 protagonists?

The two central protagonists in Grand Theft Auto VI are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Rockstar also names Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre’Quan Priest, the Real Dimez duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder among the people of Leonida.

Protagonist

Jason Duval

Jason grew up around grifters and crooks. After a stint in the Army, he found himself in the Keys working for local drug runners. Meeting Lucia could change the direction of his life.

Protagonist

Lucia Caminos

Lucia is determined to build the good life her mother dreamed of. After time in the Leonida Penitentiary, she is focused on taking smart moves into her own hands.

Jason’s friend

Cal Hampton

Jason’s friend and a fellow associate of Brian’s, Cal feels safest at home. He snoops on Coast Guard communications and sees trouble everywhere.

Vice City entrepreneur

Boobie Ike

A Vice City local legend, Boobie has turned his time in the streets into a business empire spanning real estate, a strip club and a recording studio.

Music business

Dre’Quan Priest

Dre’Quan was always more hustler than gangster. He is focused on breaking into music and the Vice City scene with Only Raw Records.

Music duo

Real Dimez: Bae-Luxe & Roxy

Friends since high school, Bae-Luxe and Roxy turn viral videos, rap tracks and social media momentum into a shot at fame.

Bank robber

Raul Bautista

A confident, seasoned bank robber who is always looking for talent willing to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards.

Keys smuggler

Brian Heder

A veteran smuggler who still moves product through his boat yard in the Keys. Jason lives rent-free at one of Brian’s properties in return for help with local shakedowns.

Where they fit

Connections across Leonida.

Jason is connected to Cal Hampton and Brian Heder. Lucia is his partner in the story’s central criminal conspiracy. Boobie Ike and Dre’Quan Priest are tied to Vice City’s entertainment business, while Bae-Luxe and Roxy perform as Real Dimez. Raul Bautista is a seasoned robber seeking people willing to take risks.

Rockstar may reveal more characters and details. This guide distinguishes announced information from speculation and does not present the roster as complete.
